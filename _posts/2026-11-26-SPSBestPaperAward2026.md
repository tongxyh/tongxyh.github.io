Our paper [End-to-End Learnt Image Compression via Non-Local Attention Optimization and Improved Context Modeling](https://ieeexplore.ieee.org/document/9359473) (Tong Chen, Haojie Liu, Zhan Ma, Qiu Shen, Xun Cao, and Yao Wang), published in *IEEE Transactions on Image Processing*, vol. 30, 2021, has been selected for the **2026 IEEE Signal Processing Society Best Paper Award**. Congratulations to all co-authors! The code is available at [NIC](https://github.com/NJUVISION/NIC).
